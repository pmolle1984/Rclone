Privacy Policy for Rclone

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Access

Rclone uses Google OAuth to access only the specific Google Drive files, folders, and metadata explicitly authorized by you during the consent process.

2. How We Use Information

Accessed data is used strictly to synchronize, transfer, and manage your files between your local client and Google Drive. We do not collect, sell, rent, or trade your personal data or Google user data to third parties.

3. Data Storage and Security

OAuth tokens are stored locally on your machine within your Rclone configuration file and are never transmitted to external servers other than Google's official APIs. You can revoke access at any time via your Google Account Permissions.

4. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact pmolle@gmail.com.
